# Primary-source research and control mapping

Author: Jason Stys. Sources checked 2026-10-14. Guidance paraphrased; corpus/code original. Educational mapping, not certification or full framework coverage.

- [OWASP prompt injection](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm01-prompt-injection/): direct/indirect content manipulation motivates separate scenario origins. Here only downstream proposals are tested.
- [OWASP excessive agency](https://genai.owasp.org/llmrisk/llm062025-excessive-agency/): minimal tools/permissions, user-context authority, human approval and complete mediation outside the model motivate the broker.
- [OWASP prevention cheat sheet](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/LLM_Prompt_Injection_Prevention_Cheat_Sheet.html): layered boundaries and objective-specific observable grading motivate explicit pending/inconclusive and tool-completion metrics.
- [OWASP GenAI risk index](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/): supplied 2025 identifiers LLM01/02/05/06/08/10 inform scope. Marker/citation checks do not fully solve disclosure or RAG security.
- [NIST AI 600-1 Generative AI Profile](https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence): voluntary risk-management companion. Reproducible context/provenance and explicit limits support measurement practice; no conformance/government authorization is claimed.
- [MITRE ATLAS](https://atlas.mitre.org/): adversary knowledge base including prompt injection and AI agent tool invocation informs scenario selection; no technique-coverage percentage asserted.

Use this as evidence of defensive engineering, not clearance or deployment approval. Real data classification, organizational authorization, procurement and compliance require separate review.
